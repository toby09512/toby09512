### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Electrical Engineering undergraduate** specializing in the intersection of **Radio Frequency (RF) Signal Processing**, **Edge AI Inference**, and **Cybersecurity Operations**. 

Unlike pure software developers, my background bridges **hardware-level communication protocols** (SDR, embedded systems, logic circuits) with **high-performance AI deployment** and **automated threat triage**. I focus on building lightweight, low-latency tools that solve real-world engineering and security challenges.

- 📡 **Current Flagship Project:** Building a real-time **2.4 GHz RF Spectrum Threat Detector** powered by **HackRF One**, STFT waterfall generation, and **YOLOv8 accelerated with TensorRT FP16** on **NVIDIA Jetson Orin** (~24 FPS real-time inference).
- 🛡️ **Cybersecurity & CTF:** Active member of campus cybersecurity community (`nighthawk-isc`), CTF challenge author (Cryptography & Steganography), and preparing for **EC-Council E|CIH (Certified Incident Handler)**.
- ⚙️ **Engineering Philosophy:** Automate repetitive packet/log triage, optimize edge compute bottlenecks, and maintain strict Operational Security (OPSEC) in every codebase.
